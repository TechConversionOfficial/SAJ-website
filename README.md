## About the business

SAJ Multispares manufactures and exports parts used in the automotive and wider industrial supply chain. Public company information identifies **spring pins, king pins, trailer pins, equalizer bolts, torque rods, axle seats, bushes, U-bolts, wheel bolts, sleeves, washers, container lock pins and custom components** among its product offerings.

Its potential buyers include automotive OEMs, replacement-parts distributors, manufacturers of trucks and buses, trailer builders, tractor and construction-equipment businesses, and international buyers sourcing components to drawing or sample.

The company's public profile also describes CNC machining, grinding, threading, surface finishing and export-oriented packing and logistics support. These are **company-reported capabilities**, not independently audited manufacturing processes or specifications.

The business is associated with **Ludhiana, Punjab** and uses **sales@sajmultispares.com** for commercial contact according to its company listing. Current telephone numbers, manufacturing-site addresses and quality certificates should be verified with the company before being reused elsewhere.

## The problem

An industrial buyer does not evaluate a supplier using product photographs alone. Purchasing and engineering teams need to identify the correct part, material, dimension, tolerance, surface treatment, application and ability to manufacture to drawings. A distributor may be comparing an entire suspension-parts range, while an OEM may need a specialised component and a quote tied to a technical specification.

For a business with products spanning commercial vehicles, industrial machinery and other demanding applications, the website needs to make **product discovery and manufacturing credibility** easier to understand. It should also offer a simple way to communicate drawings or requirements without forcing technical enquiries into a generic retail-style purchase flow.

This is the business communication challenge reflected by the company profile and Tech Conversion's portfolio positioning. It is **not** an independently audited assessment of an earlier production website.

## How the website project is positioned

Tech Conversion presents SAJ as a manufacturing website focused on **precision automotive components, manufacturing capabilities and export-ready engineering expertise**. The following priorities explain that positioning without inventing an internal build process or claiming unverified production functionality.

1. **Lead with industrial manufacturing expertise.** Make the company's engineering and component-production focus clear to visitors arriving from search, referrals or procurement enquiries.
2. **Organise a broad product range.** Distinguish spring pins and king pins from trailer components, fasteners, bushes, bolts and specialised parts so buyers can narrow their requirements.
3. **Help technical buyers assess suitability.** Make room for product drawings, dimensions, materials, finish, application and part references wherever the manufacturer has authorised specifications.
4. **Present capabilities alongside parts.** Connect CNC machining, grinding, threading and surface finishing to the practical needs of customers ordering standard and custom components.
5. **Support custom manufacturing enquiries.** Buyers seeking parts made from drawings or samples should be able to communicate their exact requirements to the sales or engineering team.
6. **Explain relevant applications.** The company's products serve trucks, buses, trailers, tractors, construction equipment, industrial machines and other sectors; this context helps procurement teams find appropriate component families.
7. **Address international sourcing concerns.** An export-oriented B2B website should provide clear contact routes and appropriate information about packaging, documentation, lead times and commercial discussions, subject to confirmed company policies.
8. **Keep technical and quality claims traceable.** Claims about standards, tolerances, testing, manufacturing capacity and certifications must be checked against actual client documentation before being presented as verified achievements.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

The following are **appropriate evaluation criteria**, not claims that SAJ passed any independent certification, audit or testing.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** evaluate homepage, image-heavy component galleries and mobile loading performance.
- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** review keyboard operation, form labelling, contrast, focus visibility and meaningful descriptions of part photographs.
- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** check discoverable product families, descriptive manufacturing content, useful headings and clear internal linking.
- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** assess accurate Organisation, Product, Service and BreadcrumbList markup when supported by the published content.
- **Engineering accuracy:** confirm drawings, materials, material grades, tolerances, hardness, dimensions, finishes and applications against authorised product records.
- **Quality standards:** do not imply an ISO or IATF certification without the company's current certificate, certification body and scope.
- **B2B export enquiries:** review handling of submitted drawings, commercial information, purchaser contact details and applicable privacy and export requirements.
